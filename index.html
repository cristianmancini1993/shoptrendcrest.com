How ordering works

Four steps from choosing a product to holding it in your hands.

  1. 1. Choose a productOpen the product page of your choice and read the details, the contents of the package and the price.
  2. 2. Leave your detailsFill in the short form with your name, phone number and delivery address. No card number and no prepayment are requested.
  3. 3. Confirm by phoneOur team calls you to confirm the order and the address, in your language, so nothing is lost in translation.
  4. 4. Pay on deliveryThe courier brings the parcel in 24-48 hours. You check it, and you pay in cash only when you receive it.

Delivery, payment and returns

Delivery takes 24-48 hours in most areas and shipping is free. You do not pay anything when you place the order: the courier collects the payment in cash when the parcel is handed over, and you can check the package before you pay.

Every order is covered by a 30-day refund and by a 24-month manufacturer warranty against defects. The complete terms are written in the Refund Policy, the Shipping Policy and the Terms & Conditions.

Frequently asked questions

How does cash on delivery work?

You place the order with a short form, without paying anything. The courier delivers the parcel to your door and you pay in cash when you receive it. If you change your mind at the door, you can refuse the delivery.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are shipped quickly and arrive in 24-48 hours in most areas. Shipping is free, so the price you see on the product page is the price you pay.

Can I return a product?

Yes. Every order is covered by a 30-day refund. The full conditions are written in our Refund Policy, which you can open from the footer of this page.

Is there a warranty?

Products come with a 24-month manufacturer warranty that covers defects. If something is wrong, contact customer service and we will help you.

Why do the product pages open in another language?

We deliver to 18 European countries and every product has local order pages with local support. The product you choose opens in the language of the country where its order page is hosted. The offer and the 50% price are the same.

How can I contact you?

Write to us from the Contact page or by email, and our customer service will answer in your language. You will find the address of the company in the footer.
